#4471 · two problems, not one

owner hajpsonresolvedreviewed 2026-11-02 · 0 days ago

Since April, people have told us their bookings land on the wrong day. By 4 October the desk had 412 reports, merged as one ticket. They are not one bug. The kb logic splits them into two problems, one per cause. The desk has now worked both.

The two causes

causereportstenantsseen
parser379112026-04-01 → 2026-10-04
hint3332026-04-17 → 2026-09-22

#1 — Date parser flips day-first tenants' dates. 379 reports. The shared parser reads every date month first; a day-first tenant's DD/MM/YYYY comes out flipped.

#2 — booking-v3 form hint misreads day-first tenants' dates. 33 reports. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY and reads the tenant's typed date that way; only the three booking-v3 tenants hit it.

Lessons

  • A merged ticket can hold more than one cause. Count by cause, not by subject.
  • The tenant's booking form decides which bug a report is, not the intake tag.
